Round S4 · Body record (Stage 4) · synthetic data only

Every number here is invented. "Fixture Person" is the one fictional subject of every stage (stage 1 specification §13). The three scans, the benchmarks and the blood results are the stage 4 specification's fixture (§13); each scan is dated on a stage 1 weigh-in day and carries that day's weight as its total mass. Each frame is that fixture, or one of its named variants, run through a design model of the specified rules (model.js) and worded with the specification's strings (frames.js). These screens draw no chart and colour no value: figures are in the primary text colour, a change carries its sign and a direction word, and the only semantic colour is red on Delete and on an entry that cannot be saved.
