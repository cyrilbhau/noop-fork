Round 5 · Your cards on Today · synthetic data

Your cards is the user's own pick of metrics and shortcuts, drawn as a two-column grid of tiles so it reads differently from Key metrics (compact rows with a trend line) and Added cards (full cards mirrored from Sleep, Trends and Stress). Tiles follow the saved order exactly. Metric tiles show one number, its unit and an honest caption. Shortcut tiles (Coupled view, Coach) show no number. Section titles sit on the 16pt gutter (spacing plan, 29 Oct).
