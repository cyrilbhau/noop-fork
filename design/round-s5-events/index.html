Round S5 · Events layer (Stage 5) · synthetic data only

Every number, date and event here is invented. "Fixture Person" is the fictional subject of the stage 1 specification's fixture (§13): a cut from Mon 6 Jan 2031, weighed on Wednesdays, with week 10 missed. Stage 5 adds eight events to that person (§13.1). Each frame is that fixture on a stated day, run through a design model of the specified overlap and presenter rules (model.js), then worded with the specification's strings. An event has no colour: it is a small grey dot on the time axis and a line of text. See README.md for the frame list and the decisions being asked.
