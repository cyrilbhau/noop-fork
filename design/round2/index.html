06 · How the background reacts
07 · Motion spec

Motion & loading

Springs everywhere, nothing linear. Every fresh load replays the fill so the screen feels alive. Missing data never animates: it stays an empty dashed ring with a dash.
Skeleton shimmer

Card-shaped placeholders with faint text bars and a 110° light sweep, 1.25 s loop. Shown only while data is resolving; kept at least 300 ms so it never flickers.

Card entrance

Top to bottom, 40 ms stagger. Rise 16 pt, scale 0.98 → 1. Spring response 0.45 · damping 0.78.

Ring fill

Every appearance and refresh: 0 → value, spring response 0.9 · damping 0.72, overshoot capped at 4%. Number counts up in step. Soft haptic on settle.

Arc knob

Rides the Recovery arc and settles with a small bounce. Colour crossfades through the band it passes (red → yellow → green).

Charts

Line draws left to right 700 ms ease-out. Gradient fill fades in 200 ms later. Latest-point dot pops 0 → 1.15 → 1.

Ambient field

Background blobs are coloured and sized by the day's numbers. They crossfade 800 ms on date change or new scores, and drift slowly while idle.

Pull to refresh

Rings drain to zero as you pull, then refill on release. The strap battery pill pulses while syncing.

Press

Cards and buttons scale to 0.965 on touch-down (120 ms), spring back on release. Chevrons nudge 2 pt toward their destination.

Day swipe

Interactive horizontal spring. The incoming day's rings refill and the ambient field crossfades to that day's colours.

Live heart rate

Heart icon pulses at the measured BPM. Each new reading grows the newest bar from zero.

Tab switch

Native iOS 26 tab bar; symbol bounce on select, 200 ms content crossfade, no remount of the tab's state.

Reduce Motion

Everything becomes a 150 ms fade. No drift, no overshoot, no pulsing heart.
